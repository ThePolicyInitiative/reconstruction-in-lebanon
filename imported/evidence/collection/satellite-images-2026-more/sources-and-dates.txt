LEBANON: MORE SATELLITE IMAGES FROM 2026
Compiled 7 October 2026

Ten atlas pages; 16 source image files containing 26 panels. Twenty-three panels are dated 2026; three are explicitly marked August 2025 reference views.
Latest individually dated image in this collection: 22 August 2026.
Original downloaded files are retained. The PDF compresses images for viewing but adds no damage markings or AI reconstructions.
This is an image collection, not an independent count of buildings or destroyed land area.
Some composites overlay dated satellite coverage on an older background mosaic. Dates refer to survey coverage, not every background pixel.
Provider credits follow each publisher; Vantor watermarks visible in the Al Jazeera composites are preserved.
