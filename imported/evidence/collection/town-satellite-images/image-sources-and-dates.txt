LEBANON: TOWN SATELLITE IMAGE COLLECTION
Compiled 7 October 2026

Nine towns; 18 original image files containing 22 dated satellite panels.
This collection supplements the earlier Lebanon destruction comparison.
Images retain publisher annotations, dates and credits. No AI reconstruction, retouching or inferred building counts have been added.
Earlier images may already contain damage. Dates do not describe the condition on 7 October 2026.
Changes in colour, vegetation, shadow and image resolution are not independently proof of destruction.
