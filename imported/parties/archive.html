Positions on reconstruction

What political figures say, the conditions they attach, and how their positions develop.

Party comparisons

Hezbollah

Statements and sources

Naim Qassem · Hassan Fadlallah · Ali Fayyad

Qassem rejects arms-linked conditionality. Fadlallah emphasizes shelter and phased state repairs. Ali Fayyad urges initial work without waiting for foreign aid. These are complementary emphases in the sample.

Amal

Statements and sources

Nabih Berri · Ali Hassan Khalil · Inaya Ezzeddine

Berri and Ali Hassan Khalil reject political bargaining over reconstruction. Inaya Ezzeddine emphasizes state protection and rapid school rebuilding. Her education priorities do not establish disagreement over aid conditions.

Lebanese Forces

Statements and sources

Samir Geagea · Ghassan Hasbani · Pierre Bou Assi

Geagea, Hasbani and Bou Assi link reconstruction to state authority and arms control. Bou Assi specifies state aid channels; Hasbani additionally demands an Iranian contribution to costs.

Free Patriotic Movement

Statements and sources

Gebran Bassil · Ghassan Atallah

Bassil rejects arms-linked conditionality but objects to borrowing without a plan. Ghassan Atallah demands a reconstruction budget allocation. Different financing emphases do not by themselves establish disagreement.

Kataeb

Statements and sources

Samy Gemayel · Salim Sayegh · Elias Hankach

Gemayel and Sayegh emphasize arms control and donor confidence. Hankach adds transparency requirements; his March and July descriptions of state responsibility differ and need context before calling a reversal.

Progressive Socialist Party

Statements and sources

Walid Joumblatt · Timour Joumblatt

Walid Joumblatt links reconstruction to reform. Timour Joumblatt calls for international support, later endorsing a package of withdrawal, state arms control, return and reconstruction. Walid is the former party leader.

Marada Movement

Statements and sources

Tony Frangieh

Tony Frangieh calls for reconstruction and return as presidential priorities, alongside support for the army and diplomacy. No explicit aid-withholding condition is stated. One speaker only.

Future Movement

Statements and sources

Method, source access and coverage gaps
Attribution
One dated statement by one named speaker. Attendance alone is not a statement. Party grouping does not convert a personal statement into a formal resolution.
Conditions
Separate own prerequisites, reported donor conditions, reciprocal packages, project safeguards, practical obstacles and explicit rejection of conditionality. No condition stated is not the same as unconditional support.
Changes over time
Compare the same speaker and scope. Silence on an earlier condition does not prove abandonment. Single observations cannot establish change.
Dates and scope
Event dates and publication dates are distinguished where known. Shelter, repairs, compensation and full reconstruction can have different conditions. The archive includes the renewed conflict in 2026.
Source access
Every statement links to the evidence used. “Indexed text” means search-index text was readable during research, even if the original page could not be opened directly. Published reporting and speaker claims have not all been independently verified. Short excerpts retain their original language; some English excerpts are source translations.
Party affiliation
Grouping does not turn a personal statement into a party resolution. Tashnag coverage uses an electoral and bloc affiliate; formal membership is not asserted. Roles reflect the cited period. Other uncertain affiliations are flagged on individual profiles.
Missing coverage
Second speakers for Marada, LCP, SSNP, NLP, Jamaa Islamiya, Tashnag, Taqaddom, Karama, Union Party and Popular Nasserist Organization. Lebanese Democratic Party, Arab Tawhid, other unrepresented parties and many independent MPs. Tashnag leadership statements and competing SSNP currents. Longer time series for speakers with one statement, and full broadcasts underlying several reports. Comprehensive donor timelines. Missing coverage is not evidence of silence.
Freshness
Latest collected means the newest entry here, not necessarily the speaker’s latest public position. The sample includes renewed conflict in 2026.
